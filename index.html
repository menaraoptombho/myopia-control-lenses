The core idea

Why single-vision glasses aren't enough

In a myopic eye, light entering straight ahead can be focused sharply at the centre of the retina — but light entering at an angle often focuses behind the peripheral retina. That mismatch is now understood as a signal that can drive the eye to keep growing, and the myopia to keep worsening.

The science, simplified

Three ways a lens can slow eye growth

Despite different names, most myopia control lenses work by reshaping how light focuses at the edges of the retina, not just the centre.

Ring & lenslet arrays

Hundreds of tiny aspherical lenslets, arranged in concentric rings around a clear central zone, create a controlled myopic defocus signal at the peripheral retina. Used by Essilor Stellest.

Honeycomb defocus segments

A honeycomb grid of small myopic-defocus segments surrounds a clear central optical zone for distance vision. Used in Hoya MiyoSmart's D.I.M.S. design.

Cylindrical annular zones

Concentric annular zones with cylindrical power reshape peripheral focus while keeping the central zone clear. Used in Zeiss MyoCare's C.A.R.E. technology.

Questions

Frequently asked

What age can a child start on a myopia control lens?

Most designs are suitable from around age 6–8 once a child can be fitted and measured accurately, though some ranges (like Zeiss MyoCare) offer age-specific versions. Your optometrist will confirm the right fit and timing.

Do these lenses need to be worn all day?

Manufacturer data for lenses like Stellest and MiyoSmart is based on a minimum of around 12 hours of daily wear. Consistent, full-time wear is generally what clinical efficacy figures assume.

Can these lenses be combined with blue light or photochromic coatings?

Yes — most ranges here offer a clear option, a blue-light-filtering option, and in some cases photochromic finishes (such as Sensity, Tinted Sunbird, or Photofusion).

Which lens is "the best" one?

There isn't a single best lens for every child — the right choice depends on prescription, age, lifestyle and how the eyes respond over time. This page is a starting point for comparison; a proper fitting and follow-up with an eye care practitioner is what determines suitability.
